# Talent Acquisition — Direct Hiring Channels

No private phone/mail scraping here (privacy + spam risk). Use official channels below + your own contacts.

- `hiring-contacts.md` — official careers + talent pages per company (add your referral contacts privately, do NOT commit personal numbers)
- `email-templates.md` — cold mail + LinkedIn DM templates
- `my-contacts-template.csv` — local-only template (gitignored) for your own manager list

Rule: never commit personal numbers/mails to GitHub. Keep `my-contacts.csv` local.
